Before you connect

Telewish links two browsers directly — no server relaying your messages. Your chat is encrypted twice: once by WebRTC's built-in DTLS, and again with a fresh AES-256 key derived from the passphrase you speak to each other.

  • Your IP address is visible to the other person.A direct connection means their device sees your network address — the same as a video call. Use a trustworthy no-log VPN if you want to hide it.
  • No one is moderating this.There's no company, server, or report button behind Telewish — anything said here is between the two of you alone.
  • Only connect with people you trust.The code and passphrase together are the keys. Share each half over a different channel — one on a screen, the other spoken aloud.
  • Nothing is saved unless you ask for it.By default, closing the tab ends the conversation for good. You can opt in to keeping chats on this device only — they never leave your machine.
  • Use a VPN for maximum privacy.A VPN hides your real IP from your peer and from the STUN server used for the handshake. When both sides use a trustworthy no-log VPN, the other person sees only the VPN's address. This is the strongest simple upgrade — and unlike a relay, it adds almost no latency.

You'll only see this once a day on this device.

Start or join a chat

Telewish splits the connection code in half: one half is a code (QR or text), the other half is a short passphrase you share through a different channel. Both halves are needed — and they also salt the AES-256 key that encrypts every message on top of DTLS.
